## Acceptance assertions

The focused provider suite establishes all of the following:

1. Abby provider imports and capability lookup perform no network call, model
   import, or credential requirement;
2. `abby_indextts` is synthesis-only and `abby_whisper` is
   transcription-only, with canonical aliases and lazy construction;
3. IndexTTS request JSON, endpoint, headers, model, voice, format, options,
   timeout, direct audio, base64 audio, and safe audio-download forms are
   normalized;
4. Whisper byte/file input, content type, quoted model URL, language, billing,
   authorization, timeout, and nested output forms are normalized;
5. empty inputs, empty audio, malformed JSON/base64, missing result fields,
   unsafe cross-origin downloads, and terminal HTTP failures are rejected;
6. transient statuses retry exactly within the bound and terminal statuses do
   not retry or trip a circuit;
7. endpoint order, injected capped backoff, finite timeout propagation, and
   success short-circuit are deterministic;
8. circuits open after the configured exhausted-call threshold, fast-fail
   without I/O, admit a cooldown probe, close/reset on success, and reopen on
   failure;
9. endpoint and credential configuration isolate cache identity without
   exposing credential text;
10. the router records exact remote/local call order, capability filtering,
    failed/successful traces, selected fallback provider, degradation reason,
    content hashes, and embedded provider receipt;
11. an explicit chain containing no capable provider remains authoritative
    and returns text-only instead of silently selecting an automatic provider;
12. all-STT failure produces a failed safe-handoff receipt and all-TTS failure
    produces text-only output;
13. Bearer, query, and assignment credentials plus reflected caller
    input are redacted; raw audio and generated bytes are absent from default
    JSON;
14. coroutine results at the synchronous provider/transport boundary are
    closed and rejected without a warning or nested event-loop behavior;
15. every test uses synthetic audio and injected transports/providers and
    calls no paid, mutable, or real remote service.
